What's inside

Four bears

Honey, polar, koala or panda. Each one has its own calculator colors, and every bear reacts to the keys you press.

Three keypads

Simple big keys for little hands, a standard layout, or a classic pocket calculator with memory.

Gentle sounds

Four soft sound sets (Bloop, Forest, Toybox and Hum), with a volume slider and mute.

Tap the bear

The bear wakes up when you start and clears the calculator when you tap it, with a happy giggle.

Hold to copy

Press and hold the display to copy the number, just like any other calculator.

A real calculator

Add, subtract, multiply and divide, a percent key that works like a pocket calculator, and an 8-digit LCD-style display.

Before you install

Who is it for?

Children who are getting to know numbers, and grown-ups who would like a calculator that makes them smile.

Is it free? Are there ads?

It is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases, so it is fine to hand to a child.

Does it need an internet connection or an account?

No. Everything runs on the phone, with no sign-in, and it works offline.

Does it collect any data?

No. The app only keeps your own settings (bear, keypad and sound) on your phone. See the privacy policy.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Bears & Co is available for Android on Google Play, and you can use it in your browser on any device here.
