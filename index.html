What's inside

Four bears

Honey, polar, koala or panda. Each one has its own calculator colors, and every bear reacts to the keys you press.

Three keypads

Simple big keys for little hands, a standard layout, or a classic pocket calculator with memory.

Gentle sounds

Four soft sound sets (Bloop, Forest, Toybox and Hum), with a volume slider and mute.

Tap the bear

The bear wakes up when you start and clears the calculator when you tap it, with a happy giggle.

Hold to copy

Press and hold the display to copy the number, just like any other calculator.
